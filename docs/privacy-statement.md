# Privacy statement (draft)

**Status:** draft, written 12 November 2026. It describes the email feature in spec criterion 28, which is **not built**. Do not publish it as `public/privacy.html` until the blanks in square brackets are filled in and the owner has read it. It must be true on the day the first email goes. This is not legal advice: check it against the Australian Privacy Act and the Spam Act 2003 before launch.

Facts the owner has decided: the address is used once (H1), sending the card does not create an account, and no printing from the phone. Everything in square brackets depends on D4 (which email service) and is not decided.

---

## Text for the page

### How we use your email

TRI Momentum can email you your race card as a PDF. You give us your email address for that. This page says what we send, what we keep, and what we don't.

### What we send

We send two things to our email service, [service name, country].

- **Your email address.**
- **Your race card.** The race name, date and distance, your pace targets and finish range, and your nutrition plan: carbs an hour, gel times and drinks. It also carries the "not medical advice" note.

The service sends you one email with the PDF.

### What we don't send

Anything that isn't on your race card stays on your phone. That includes your body weight, your fitness test results and your sweat rate.

We use no analytics. We set no cookies. We don't track you.

### What stays on your phone

Your plan stays in your phone's browser storage. It never left your phone before, and it still doesn't, apart from the card you ask us to send.

We don't save your email address on your phone. We use it once, to send your card. It goes when you leave the page.

### What we keep

[To confirm with the email service: what it keeps (the address, the message, a delivery record), where, and for how long.]

We keep no list of addresses. We don't use your address for anything else. We don't sell it or share it.

### No account

Adding your email doesn't create an account. We don't ask you to make one.

### Your choices

You don't need to give an email. Your plan works on your phone without one.

If our email service keeps a record of your address, you can ask us to delete it. Write to [contact address].

### Who we are

TRI Momentum, [legal name and contact].

### Changes

If this changes, we update this page and its date. Last updated: [date of launch].
