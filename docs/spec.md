# Spec: TRI Momentum, the race-day tools (first build)

Written 3 November 2026 from the Notion strategy page "Strategy: Race-day tool suite" (RD-000), the two tool pages ("Race Fuel Planner", "Race Pacing Planner") and the blueprint of 2 November 2026 (strategy, journey, set-up flow, screens F1–F7, A1, A2, C1). Card keys such as `RP-05-W2` refer to that blueprint.

Revised 9 November 2026 for the four-step flow (owner's Race-Day Blueprint, level 3 "Set-up flow", approved 8–9 November 2026). The steps are 1 Choose a race, 2 Choose pacing, 3 Review nutrition and 4 Race card. Code names such as `fuel.js` and `#/fuel` are older than the word "nutrition" and stay.

## Objective

One static web app that holds the first two race-day tools, the Pacing Planner and the Fuel Planner (shown as nutrition). They share the athlete's details and write to one race card. Pace splits feed the nutrition plan.

## Acceptance criteria

Each is checkable. The tests that cover each one are named.

1. **Static and on-device.** `public/` runs from any static server with no build step. No accounts, no analytics. The app makes two kinds of network request and no other, both same-origin POSTs made only when the person taps: `/api/send`, to email their race card (criterion 29), and `/api/feedback`, to send a feedback message (criterion 31). State lives in `localStorage` under one key. Every storage read and write survives storage being blocked. Fonts ship with the app in `public/fonts/`,, and the content policy names no outside host (`connect-src 'self'`, `form-action 'none'`), so no page requests anything from another host. On GitHub Pages, where `/api/send` does not exist, the email block and the feedback footer are hidden (criteria 29 and 31) and the app works in full; on any other static host without the function it says plainly that email is not working. (`store.js`; `pages.test.js` "the content policy names no outside host", "the three content-policy tags are identical, and allow only same-origin requests", "no file in public/ names the mail service or holds a key" and "every font file app.css names is shipped"; smoke test reload check, outside-request guard, `/api/` guard (which allows POST `/api/feedback` as well as POST `/api/send`), content-policy probe and fonts-load check)
2. **The four steps.** Step 1, Choose a race: race name, distance (Sprint, Olympic, 70.3 or Ironman) and date. It shows no countdown and no taper choices: both start at step 2, in the race strip (criteria 20 and 30). Step 2, Choose pacing, in three parts with a part bar ("Pacing · part n of 3"): Your fitness (the Power / Heart rate / Effort choice with a line saying what each mode needs and that the swim always uses CSS; CSS, with a line saying what it is and why we ask; the bike fields for the mode; run threshold; body weight, with the line "Turns your power into a bike time. It does not change your nutrition plan."), Course and heat, and Your targets. Step 3, Review nutrition: it opens with gut and sweat. Step 4, the race card. (smoke test)
3. **Plausible inputs only (RP-03-A1).** Next refuses out-of-range or unreadable numbers, with a message by the field. A blank field is never read as zero. On step 3 a gut figure that is unreadable or outside 0 to 120, and an own sweat rate (typical not chosen) that is blank, unreadable or outside 0.2 to 3, show a message by the field once the person leaves it. The plan is not made from any of them, none is read as zero, and none is quietly swapped for the typical rate. A message goes as soon as what is typed reads, and a new one waits for the person to leave the field or press Next. Times can be typed as digits ("130" is 1:30, "245" is 2:45) or with a dot ("1.30"), and the field shows the time it read when the person leaves it. One or two digits ("45"), seconds or minutes of 60 or more, and a row of zeros are refused, never guessed, with a message that says what to type. A comma is not read. (`pace.test.js` "implausible numbers"; `fuel.test.js` "blank gut figure"; `time.test.js`; smoke test pacing part 1 and step 3; gut and sweat errors show by their fields on step 3)
4. **A range, never one time (RD-05-A2).** Every leg time and the finish are ranges. (`pace.test.js` "legs always add up")
5. **The numbers add up (RP-05-W2).** The finish range is exactly the sum of the displayed leg minutes and changeovers. (`pace.test.js`)
6. **Blueprint worked example.** 70.3, FTP 240 W, CSS 1:45, run threshold 4:50 /km, 72 kg, rolling, warm gives a bike target of 175–185 W and a run of 5:15–5:30 /km. (`pace.test.js`; smoke test pacing part 3)
7. **"How we got these" (RP-05-E3)** is open by default on pacing part 3.
8. **Pace feeds nutrition (RD-05-A1, RP-06-A2).** Step 3's subtitle is "Built from your pacing. Change anything that looks wrong." when the times come from pacing, and "Add your bike and run times. Change anything that looks wrong." when there is no pace plan. The first save of the targets (the race has neither pace nor nutrition) opens step 3 and shows "Pace targets saved to your race card"; there is no separate saved screen. Step 3 shows, from top to bottom: the swim, bike and run tiles in the race card's style (the big time is the middle of each pace range, which is what the plan uses; the small line under it is the pace in the middle of that range: about 1:45 /100m for the swim, 34 km/h for the bike and 4:50 /km for the run, and "h:mm" for a plan with no readable pace); "Your gut and sweat", with the heat choice (criterion 23); one "Your nutrition" card holding the carb pickers, the line saying what the plan uses and the carry list; the plan written as leg cards (criterion 24); the Gel timing card (criterion 22); and the cap and safety note last. A saved nutrition plan is marked out of date when anything it was made from changes: splits, details, products, picks, heat or gel times. A saved plan whose own sweat rate has since gone blank, unreadable or out of range is not called out of date for that alone: home and the race card say "Add your sweat rate on step 3, or use a typical value." beside any out-of-date notice, never instead of it, and step 3 makes no plan until the rate is valid. (smoke test)
9. **Gut cap (RD-02-A1, RP-02-A2).** Planned carbs per hour, drink and gels together, never pass the gut figure on any leg, for any product. A drink too strong on its own is diluted with water, with a warning. (`fuel.test.js`)
10. **Fluid cap (RD-02-W1).** Planned fluid never passes the sweat rate. (`fuel.test.js`)
11. **Own products (RD-02-A3).** People can add, edit and remove gels, chews and drinks. The starter products are generic, with no brand names. Carbs per serve is picked from a list of 20 to 50 g in 5 g steps, or typed exactly under Other. A saved figure that is not on the list shows as it is under Other and is never rounded to a step. On the nutrition step the athlete picks the carbs, not the product (RD-009, decided 4 Nov 2026): a gel of 20, 25, 30 or 40g, and a drink of 20, 30, 40 or 60g per serve or water only, with the unit in the label. The product's own figure from My products is offered too, so a label that says 22g stays selectable. The pick replaces the product's carbs only. The plan keeps the first gel's and first drink's name and sodium, and the pick is part of the plan input, so a saved plan goes out of date when it changes. A pick never lifts the gut cap. The product a person picks is named in the nutrition plan leg cards and in the race card text, so the choice shows where it is used. A line under the choice on the nutrition step says which gel and drink the plan uses and their carbs per serve, with the unit joined to the figure (25g a serve, 30g per 500 ml), and leaves out a figure it cannot read. The default products are named plainly (Gel, Sports drink, Chews, 3 pieces) with no "(typical)" in the name, and an older save that still has the tag loads without it. A nutrition plan saved before the tag was dropped still holds the tagged names, so the race card, the nutrition step and the carry list show them plain (`plainName` in `fuel.js`; `fuel.test.js`, smoke test). The "Your nutrition" card says they are generic starters until the person edits their products. The ranges behind them stay marked unchecked. (smoke test)
12. **Not medical advice (RD-02-A2)** appears beside every plan: on pacing part 3, on step 3, on the race card and in the emailed PDF.
13. **Guide embeds (RD-02-A4, F7, RP-07).** A guide page runs one step on its own with no race set up, under the TRI Momentum header, and its tool says nutrition. "Save to a race plan" starts a plan and carries the guide's numbers in. The gut figure is the safety cap, so it is carried only if the person typed or edited it in the tool; the page's example figure is no longer carried (decided 9 Nov 2026). A save made before this change may already hold one, and step 3 shows it as the person's figure. There is no status row and no Race plan button on a guide page. (smoke test)
14. **No printing from the device (RD-037, decided 16 Nov 2026).** There is no Print button, no print card and no print stylesheet. The browser's own print command prints the screen as it is. The race card reaches paper only as the emailed PDF (criterion 29), which is laid out as an A4 sheet to print and cut out. (smoke test: no Print button on step 3 or the race card)
15. **Phone first.** No sideways scroll at 390 px, and none on the status row, the Race plan panel or the nutrition leg cards at 360 and 320 px, and none on S0 at 390, 360 and 320 px with every truism (criterion 16). Tap targets at least 44 px. Text goes into the page as text, never as HTML. Text in the nutrition leg cards wraps at spaces only and is never broken in the middle of a word, at 390, 360 and 320 px (smoke test, which runs in Chromium only; a check on a phone is still needed; for S0, smoke "S0-whole", which loads each of the 13 truisms).

29. **Email the race card (RD-039, decided 13 Nov 2026).** On the race card only, below the card, when pace and nutrition are both saved, the nutrition plan is not `ok: false`, and the page is not served from `github.io` or a subdomain of it, an email block has a label "Email me this race card", one `type="email"` field (`inputmode="email"`, `autocomplete="email"`), the line "We use this address once, to send this card. The capitals are required: `send.js` refuses a text string that holds the lower-case words `stream` or `obj`, and a race called "Mainstream Sprint" would otherwise not send. Like any text in a PDF the name passes the checks below, so the phishing risk the owner accepted covers it too. The PDF has no start time, no time of day, no transition time and no product names; its creation date is a fixed constant (1 Jan 2026), never the day it was drawn. A card with many gels is drawn smaller, never onto a second page. The PDF library is jsPDF, vendored in `public/js/vendor/` at a pinned version with its licence. The send function, `functions/api/send.js` (a Cloudflare Pages Function, mail through Resend behind one adapter), takes POST, JSON, same origin only, with exactly the keys `email` and `pdf`. It refuses a second address, a list, a non-PDF, a PDF over 100,000 bytes or a request over 140,000 bytes, and a PDF that is not the shape of our own card. The PDF is read the way a reader reads it: text in ( ) strings is set aside by the PDF grammar (balanced brackets, backslash escapes; an unclosed string is refused, and so is one that holds `stream` or `obj`), a < > hex string may hold only hex digits and PDF whitespace (an unterminated one is refused), a % comment is allowed only in the header, its marker line and the final %%EOF, and the header and marker line are fixed shapes (`%PDF-` and a version, then `%` and high bytes only, nothing else on either line), and then every /Name in the file, streams included, must be on an allowlist of the names our real card holds (a name with a # escape is refused, and every font must be one /Type /Font, one /Subtype /Type1 and one /BaseFont that is directly Helvetica or Helvetica-Bold). Fonts with programs, images, forms, actions, links and colour spaces all need names not on the list, so they are refused. A deny list of the same kinds of names, and an inline-image pattern, run over the raw bytes as a second layer. None of this stops phishing: text, including a web address a reader turns into a link, typed into a PDF's page text passes every check, and only a PDF built on the server would close that. The owner declined that, so the risk is accepted. Subject, body and attachment name are fixed in the function. Replies are `{ "result": "sent" | "refused" | "later" }` with status 200, 400, 403, 405, 413, 415, 429 or 503 (the mail service refusing our own key or sender, its 401 or 403, is 503 to the athlete and 502 in the log; its 422 is 400), `Cache-Control: no-store`, no cross-origin headers, and never the word "delivered". It stores nothing durable (the limiter keeps hashes of the address and visitor in memory only) and logs only a status code and a count of sends. A limit in memory (3 sends per visitor in 10 minutes, a visitor being an IPv4 address or an IPv6 /64, 3 per address an hour, 100 a day per isolate) is a best effort only; the real limit is a Cloudflare rate-limiting rule on `/api/send`, set by the owner. The mail key and sender come from the environment, never the repo. The email block is not shown on `github.io` (or an unknown host), because the function does not exist there and the address would be posted to GitHub; `canEmailHere` in `card.js` decides, and `card.test.js` tests it. (`card.test.js`; `send.test.js` including "a race name with \"stream\" or \"obj\" in it still sends"; `fuel.test.js` "the optional gel before the swim"; `pages.test.js` "no file in public/ names the mail service or holds a key"; smoke test email block scenarios)
