# Spec: TRI Momentum, the race-day tools (first build)

Written 3 November 2026 from the Notion strategy page "Strategy: Race-day tool suite" (RD-000), the two tool pages ("Race Fuel Planner", "Race Pacing Planner") and the blueprint of 2 November 2026 (strategy, journey, set-up flow, screens F1–F7, A1, A2, C1). Card keys such as `RP-05-W2` refer to that blueprint.

Revised 9 November 2026 for the four-step flow (owner's Race-Day Blueprint, level 3 "Set-up flow", approved 8–9 November 2026). The steps are 1 Choose a race, 2 Choose pacing, 3 Review nutrition and 4 Race card. Code names such as `fuel.js` and `#/fuel` are older than the word "nutrition" and stay.

## Objective

One static web app that holds the first two race-day tools, the Pacing Planner and the Fuel Planner (shown as nutrition). They share the athlete's details and write to one race card. Pace splits feed the nutrition plan.

## Acceptance criteria

Each is checkable. The tests that cover each one are named.

1. **Static and on-device.** `public/` runs from any static server with no build step. No accounts, no server calls, no analytics. State lives in `localStorage` under one key. Every storage read and write survives storage being blocked. Fonts ship with the app in `public/fonts/` and the content policy names no outside host, so no page requests anything from another host. (`store.js`; `pages.test.js` "the content policy names no outside host" and "every font file app.css names is shipped"; smoke test reload check, outside-request guard and fonts-load check)
2. **The four steps.** Step 1, Choose a race: race name, distance (Olympic or 70.3), date, days to go, taper start and taper length (criterion 20). Step 2, Choose pacing, in three parts with a part bar ("Pacing · part n of 3"): Your fitness (the Power / Heart rate / Effort choice with a line saying what each mode needs and that the swim always uses CSS; CSS, with a line saying what it is and why we ask; the bike fields for the mode; run threshold; body weight, with the line "Turns your power into a bike time. It does not change your nutrition plan."), Course and heat, and Your targets. Step 3, Review nutrition: it opens with gut and sweat. Step 4, the race card. (smoke test)
3. **Plausible inputs only (RP-03-A1).** Next refuses out-of-range or unreadable numbers, with a message by the field. A blank field is never read as zero. On step 3 a gut figure that is unreadable or outside 0 to 120, and an own sweat rate (typical not chosen) that is blank, unreadable or outside 0.2 to 3, show a message by the field once the person leaves it. The plan is not made from any of them, none is read as zero, and none is quietly swapped for the typical rate. A message goes as soon as what is typed reads, and a new one waits for the person to leave the field or press Next. Times can be typed as digits ("130" is 1:30, "245" is 2:45) or with a dot ("1.30"), and the field shows the time it read when the person leaves it. One or two digits ("45"), seconds or minutes of 60 or more, and a row of zeros are refused, never guessed, with a message that says what to type. A comma is not read. (`pace.test.js` "implausible numbers"; `fuel.test.js` "blank gut figure"; `time.test.js`; smoke test pacing part 1 and step 3; gut and sweat errors show by their fields on step 3)
4. **A range, never one time (RD-05-A2).** Every leg time and the finish are ranges. (`pace.test.js` "legs always add up")
5. **The numbers add up (RP-05-W2).** The finish range is exactly the sum of the displayed leg minutes and changeovers. (`pace.test.js`)
6. **Blueprint worked example.** 70.3, FTP 240 W, CSS 1:45, run threshold 4:50 /km, 72 kg, rolling, warm gives a bike target of 175–185 W and a run of 5:15–5:30 /km. (`pace.test.js`; smoke test pacing part 3)
7. **"How we got these" (RP-05-E3)** is open by default on pacing part 3.
8. **Pace feeds nutrition (RD-05-A1, RP-06-A2).** Step 3's subtitle is "Built from your pacing. Change anything that looks wrong." when the times come from pacing, and "Add your bike and run times. Change anything that looks wrong." when there is no pace plan. The first save of the targets (the race has neither pace nor nutrition) opens step 3 and shows "Pace targets saved to your race card"; there is no separate saved screen. Step 3 shows, from top to bottom: the swim, bike and run tiles in the race card's style (the big time is the middle of each pace range, which is what the plan uses; the small line under it is the pace in the middle of that range: about 1:45 /100m for the swim, 34 km/h for the bike and 4:50 /km for the run, and "h:mm" for a plan with no readable pace); "Your gut and sweat", with the heat choice (criterion 23); one "Your nutrition" card holding the carb pickers, the line saying what the plan uses and the carry list; the plan written as leg cards (criterion 24); the Gel timing card (criterion 22); and the cap and safety note last. A saved nutrition plan is marked out of date when anything it was made from changes: splits, details, products, picks, heat or gel times. A saved plan whose own sweat rate has since gone blank, unreadable or out of range is not called out of date for that alone: home and the race card say "Add your sweat rate on step 3, or use a typical value." beside any out-of-date notice, never instead of it, and step 3 makes no plan until the rate is valid. (smoke test)
9. **Gut cap (RD-02-A1, RP-02-A2).** Planned carbs per hour, drink and gels together, never pass the gut figure on any leg, for any product. A drink too strong on its own is diluted with water, with a warning. (`fuel.test.js`)
10. **Fluid cap (RD-02-W1).** Planned fluid never passes the sweat rate. (`fuel.test.js`)
11. **Own products (RD-02-A3).** People can add, edit and remove gels, chews and drinks. The starter products are generic, with no brand names. Carbs per serve is picked from a list of 20 to 50 g in 5 g steps, or typed exactly under Other. A saved figure that is not on the list shows as it is under Other and is never rounded to a step. On the nutrition step the athlete picks the carbs, not the product (RD-009, decided 4 Nov 2026): a gel of 20, 25, 30 or 40g, and a drink of 20, 30, 40 or 60g per serve or water only, with the unit in the label. The product's own figure from My products is offered too, so a label that says 22g stays selectable. The pick replaces the product's carbs only. The plan keeps the first gel's and first drink's name and sodium, and the pick is part of the plan input, so a saved plan goes out of date when it changes. A pick never lifts the gut cap. The product a person picks is named in the nutrition plan leg cards and in the race card text, so the choice shows where it is used (the 70 mm print card is unchanged). A line under the choice on the nutrition step says which gel and drink the plan uses and their carbs per serve, with the unit joined to the figure (25g a serve, 30g per 500 ml), and leaves out a figure it cannot read. The default products are named plainly (Gel, Sports drink, Chews, 3 pieces) with no "(typical)" in the name, and an older save that still has the tag loads without it. A nutrition plan saved before the tag was dropped still holds the tagged names, so the race card, the nutrition step and the carry list show them plain (`plainName` in `fuel.js`; `fuel.test.js`, smoke test). The "Your nutrition" card says they are generic starters until the person edits their products. The ranges behind them stay marked unchecked. (smoke test)
12. **Not medical advice (RD-02-A2)** appears beside every plan: on pacing part 3, on step 3, on the race card and on the print.
13. **Guide embeds (RD-02-A4, F7, RP-07).** A guide page runs one step on its own with no race set up, under the TRI Momentum header, and its tool says nutrition. "Save to a race plan" starts a plan and carries the guide's numbers in. The gut figure is the safety cap, so it is carried only if the person typed or edited it in the tool; the page's example figure is no longer carried (decided 9 Nov 2026). A save made before this change may already hold one, and step 3 shows it as the person's figure. There is no rail on a guide page. (smoke test)
14. **Printable top-tube card (RD-07-A2).** Print shows only a card about 7 cm wide with pace targets and nutrition per leg (g/hr, sip, gel times and km), the go-by-the-clock note and "not medical advice". Its headings say Nutrition, and an out-of-date plan says "Nutrition plan out of date. Update it before race day." It has no start time, no transition time and no time of day. (smoke test)
15. **Phone first.** No sideways scroll at 390 px, and none on the rail or the nutrition leg cards at 360 and 320 px. Tap targets at least 44 px. Text goes into the page as text, never as HTML. Text in the nutrition leg cards wraps at spaces only and is never broken in the middle of a word, at 390, 360 and 320 px (smoke test, which runs in Chromium only; a check on a phone is still needed).
16. **Home (decided 9 Nov 2026, replacing the blank card of 4 Nov).** With no race saved, or a blank one (no name, date, pace or nutrition), home is the front page (S0): the TRI Momentum wordmark, "Race with a plan.", a subtitle, the four steps as a numbered list (step 4 on the light blue), one yellow "Get started", the note "Free. No account needed. Saved on this phone only.", and at the very bottom a "Guides" label with one plain text link to each guide, each tagged Draft while its ranges are unchecked and at least 44 px high (owner decision on O1, 9 Nov 2026). It has no "Just plan my nutrition" link, no account link, no rail, and no Countdown or Debrief. Once a race has a name, date, pace or nutrition, home is the coming-back screen (S5): days to go, the race title, "<long date>. <count of steps done>", the taper line, the four steps each saying what it holds (a done step has a green tick and an Edit link of at least 44 px; the next step's number is yellow; step 4 is on the light blue until pace and nutrition are both saved), one yellow action that opens the first step not done ("Continue: choose a race", "Continue: choose pacing", "Continue: review nutrition", or "Open my race card"), a quiet "Start a new race" and "Saved on this phone only." S5 has the wordmark and no "My plan", and no guide links. A done step whose saved plan is out of date keeps its tick and says it is out of date. (smoke test)
17. **No race times (RD-019 withdrawn, 9 Nov 2026).** There is no start time and no "Transition opens" anywhere, no finish time of day, no "Add a start time" link, and no time of day on pacing part 3, home, the race card or print. New races are made without `start` or `transitionOpens`. An older save that holds them loads without error and shows neither, and is not called out of date because of them (`paceInput()` and `fuelInput()` do not read them). `time.js` no longer has `clockMinutes`, `raceTimesError`, `RACE_TIMES_MESSAGE` or `addMinutes`. (`time.test.js`; smoke test, including a save captured from the app before the change)
18. **Km beside gel times (RD-025).** Each gel time on the bike and the run shows about how far into the leg it is, marked "~", to the whole km (`kmAt` in `pace.js`, beside the leg distances): gel time ÷ leg time × leg distance, at an even pace. The time comes first and stays the rule. The km shows in the plan rows, the nutrition leg cards on step 3 and on the race card (each leg is a card, each gel a row with its time first and large and its km on the right, the leg's carbs an hour in a chip beside its name), the print card and the run line in the guide embed. One note shows under the leg cards whenever any km shows, and the print card and guide carry the same words: "Go by the clock. Never take a gel earlier than its time. Km are a guide, from an even pace." (`KM_NOTE` in `pace.js`; the owner's wording, 8–9 Nov 2026). A leg time that is blank, unreadable or zero shows the time alone, never a km or "NaN". A km never passes the leg distance. Gel times, their count, carbs per hour and the caps do not change. (`pace.test.js`, `fuel.test.js`; smoke test)
19. **The rail (RD-031, decided 5 Nov 2026; restyled 9 Nov 2026).** Step 1, every pacing part, step 3 with a race, and the race card carry a sticky rail: Race, Pacing, Nutrition and Race card, each a numbered circle, at least 44 px, and in view as the page scrolls. It is not on home, My products or step 3 with no race. The current step is marked and has `aria-current="step"`; a done step has a green circle with a tick and says ", done" to a screen reader. Step 4 sits on the light blue with a ring until it is current and never shows done in the rail. Any step opens from any other: Pacing opens the targets (part 3) once pace is saved, and part 1 before that. "My plan" in the header returns home. Once the card has a plan, step 1 and pacing parts 1 and 2 have "Save and back to race card", which works the pace targets out again. A second save on part 3 goes back to the card; only the first goes to step 3. Pace targets keep what they were made from, so the card says "Your numbers changed since these targets" when a person changes a number and leaves without saving. A plan saved before this check existed is never called out of date for that alone. The old addresses (#/setup/2 to #/setup/5, #/saved) open the matching screen. (smoke test)
20. **Taper length (RD-011, RD-031).** Under the countdown on step 1: 10, 14 or 21 days as one tap, with our guide marked (14 for a 70.3, 10 for an Olympic), or pick the day the taper starts. A picked day must fall 1 to 42 days before race day; any other day is refused with a message, stays on screen, and is not saved. Choosing our guide saves blank, never a number. The home screen and the race card show when the taper starts, or "In your taper". The print card does not: it is for race day. The guide and limits sit in `TAPER` in `pace.js`, marked UNCHECKED. (`pace.test.js` "taperDays"; smoke test)
21. **Your own T1 and T2 (RD-022, RD-031).** pacing part 3 shows T1 and T2 as their own rows, each marked "Our guide" or "Your time", and two fields under the targets. A time is whole minutes, 1 to 30; a blank uses our guide and is never zero; anything else shows a message by the field, the plan uses our guide for it, and Save waits. The finish is still exactly the legs plus the changeovers. Our split guide (`T1`, `T2` in `pace.js`, UNCHECKED) adds up to the old combined range, so a plan with no times of its own is unchanged. The race card shows the changeovers its finish includes. (`pace.test.js` "your own T1 and T2", "a T1 or T2 that is not whole minutes"; smoke test)
22. **Gel timing (RD-031).** The nutrition step has a Gel timing card, below the plan: for the bike and the run, "first gel at" (h:mm) and "then every" (whole minutes, 5 to 120), with our suggested times as the placeholders and written out underneath. Both blank uses our suggested times; one blank asks for both. No gel goes in the last 10 minutes of a leg. Times that would take carbs past the gut figure on a leg are refused with a message by the fields, and that leg keeps the suggested times: the gut cap never moves. "Use the suggested times" clears them. The athlete's times show in the leg cards, the carry list, the race card and the print card, each with its km. They are part of the plan input, so a saved plan goes out of date when they change; a plan saved before they existed does not. The race card's nutrition section links to them ("Change gel times"). (`fuel.test.js` "your own gel times"; smoke test)
23. **Gut and sweat on step 3 (decided 9 Nov 2026).** The first time, or after Change, step 3 asks two questions: the gut figure (its field starts empty, with no numeric placeholder and no default) and the sweat rate ("Typical for <heat>: <value> L/hr", read from `TYPICAL_SWEAT`, or the person's own). Until the gut figure reads there are no leg cards and no save button, and a message says what is needed. Later, the card is a summary ("Your gut handles 70 g/hr", "Sweat rate" with the typical line or the rate) with a Change link of at least 44 px. A gut figure of 0 counts as set. Weight stays in pacing. The heat choice (Mild, Warm, Hot) stays on step 3, because a race started from the nutrition guide has no pacing part 2. (smoke test; `fuel.test.js` "a blank gut figure")
24. **The nutrition plan as leg cards.** The h2 "Nutrition plan" and a summary line ("7 gels, a drink on the bike, water on the run.", whose count is the gel rows shown and which never calls chews gels), then one card per leg with the leg name and a g/hr chip; on the bike the drink line ("Sports drink: about 150 ml every 15 min", and a mix line when diluted); a row per gel with the time first and large, the product name (never "(typical)") and the km on the right; on the run "Water at each aid station"; then the bottles, sodium and top-up lines. "Before the swim" stays above the legs, and one go-by-the-clock note sits under them. The race card uses the same leg cards. (smoke test)
25. **TRI Momentum (decided 9 Nov 2026).** The tab title is "TRI Momentum", each guide title ends "· TRI Momentum", and every screen header and both guide pages show the wordmark: three ink boxes with white T, R and I, then "Momentum" in Outfit. No page says "Race-day tools" in words a person reads. (smoke test; `pages.test.js`)
26. **"Nutrition" everywhere, and Olympic.** Visible text says nutrition, not fuel, on every screen and in the guides' header and tool; the distance is labelled Olympic, never Standard, and stored as `standard`. Code names may stay. Guide article copy is out of scope. (smoke test)
27. **Saves from before 9 Nov 2026 load** and are not out of date for this change: the stored key is unchanged, `distance` stays `standard`, `paceInput()` and `fuelInput()` give the same JSON for the same state, and a fixture captured from the app at `8ba9825` is checked by the smoke test. (smoke test, `scripts/fixtures/save-before-four-step.json`)
28. **The race card header (12 Nov 2026).** The top of the race card is one ink band: the label "YOUR RACE" with an "Edit race" button; the date box, race name and long date; the countdown ("20 days to go"); and the taper line ("Taper starts Wed, 18 Nov" or "In your taper") with an "Edit taper" button. The buttons are at least 44 px high and say `aria-expanded`. "Edit race" opens a panel in the band with the race name and race date (the same fields and rules as step 1) and a Done button, and moves focus to the name. "Edit taper" opens the same taper choices as step 1 (10, 14 or 21 days with our guide marked, or the day the taper starts, refused outside 1 to 42 days before race day, never saved); it shows only when the race has a date and race day has not passed. Both save as they are typed, so the panel has Done, never Save or Cancel. The countdown stays in view and moves as the date or taper changes. Done closes the panel and returns focus to the button; a blank name becomes "My race", as on step 1. The card opens with both panels closed. No sideways scroll at 390, 360 and 320 px. Print is unchanged. (smoke test)

## Out of scope

Payments and the paywall (D6), email, including "Email as PDF" from the race card, which waits on D4 (5 Nov 2026), the gut-training ladder, Countdown, Debrief, an offline copy (RD-07-S1), analytics (D3), hosting and deployment, the SEO pipeline, and published guide copy. Countdown and Debrief are hidden everywhere (home, the race card and the rail) until they exist, so nothing promises a tool that is not there (decided 4 Nov 2026). Nutrition with no race is reached from the guide embeds only.

## Known limits

- Every range in `pace.js` and `fuel.js` is typical and **unchecked** (RP-05-W1, RD-01-W2). Verify each before launch.
- The bike time comes from a simple physics model (CdA 0.29, Crr 0.005). It lands near, not on, the blueprint example: 2:47–2:51 against 2:41–2:48.
- One race at a time. "Start a new race" replaces it and keeps the details.
- The strategy sequences Pacing after Gate 1. This build makes both now, at the owner's request (3 Nov 2026).
