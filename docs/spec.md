# Spec: Race Fuel Planner and Race Pacing Planner (first build)

Written 3 November 2026 from the Notion strategy page "Strategy: Race-day tool suite" (RD-000), the two tool pages ("Race Fuel Planner", "Race Pacing Planner") and the blueprint of 2 November 2026 (strategy, journey, set-up flow, screens F1–F7, A1, A2, C1). Card keys such as `RP-05-W2` refer to that blueprint.

## Objective

One static web app that holds the first two race-day tools, the Pacing Planner and the Fuel Planner. They share the athlete's details and write to one race card. Pace splits feed the fuel plan.

## Acceptance criteria

Each is checkable. The tests that cover each one are named.

1. **Static and on-device.** `public/` runs from any static server with no build step. No accounts, no server calls, no analytics. State lives in `localStorage` under one key. Every storage read and write survives storage being blocked. (`store.js`; smoke test reload check)
2. **Set-up flow F1–F5** follows the blueprint: F1 race, distance, date, an optional start time, an optional transition opening time, days to go, taper start and taper length (criterion 20); F2 weight (with a line saying it turns a power target into a bike time and does not change the fuel plan), sweat rate (or typical), gut tolerance; F3 CSS (critical swim speed, with a line saying what it is and why we ask), then the Power / Heart rate / Effort choice, which changes the bike and the run and never the swim and has a line saying what each mode needs, then FTP (and when tested), run threshold; F4 course and heat; F5 targets. (smoke test)
3. **Plausible inputs only (RP-03-A1).** Next refuses out-of-range or unreadable numbers, with a message by the field. A blank field is never read as zero. A message goes as soon as what is typed reads, and a new one waits for the person to leave the field or press Next. Times can be typed as digits ("130" is 1:30, "245" is 2:45) or with a dot ("1.30"), and the field shows the time it read when the person leaves it. One or two digits ("45"), seconds or minutes of 60 or more, and a row of zeros are refused, never guessed, with a message that says what to type. A comma is not read. (`pace.test.js` "implausible numbers"; `fuel.test.js` "blank gut figure"; `time.test.js`; smoke test F2 and F3)
4. **A range, never one time (RD-05-A2).** Every leg time and the finish are ranges. (`pace.test.js` "legs always add up")
5. **The numbers add up (RP-05-W2).** The finish range is exactly the sum of the displayed leg minutes and changeovers. (`pace.test.js`) When a start time is set, the targets screen, the race card and the print card also show the finish as a time of day: the start plus that finish range, to the minute, as a range marked "about", and "next day" after midnight. With no start time they show a link to add one and no time. (`time.test.js`; smoke test)
6. **Blueprint worked example.** 70.3, FTP 240 W, CSS 1:45, run threshold 4:50 /km, 72 kg, rolling, warm gives a bike target of 175–185 W and a run of 5:15–5:30 /km. (`pace.test.js`; smoke test F5)
7. **"How we got these" (RP-05-E3)** is open by default on F5.
8. **Pace feeds fuel (RD-05-A1, RP-06-A2).** Saving F5 writes targets to the race card and shows F6, whose "Plan my fuel" opens Fuel with the bike and run times filled in. The fuel step shows those times as swim, bike and run tiles in the race card's style (the big time is the middle of each pace range, which is what the plan uses; the small line under it is the pace in the middle of that range: about 1:45 /100m for the swim, 34 km/h for the bike and 4:50 /km for the run, and "h:mm" for a plan with no readable pace), and one "Your fuel" card holds the carb pickers, the line saying what the plan uses and the carry list, above the legs, with the cap and safety note last. A saved fuel plan is marked out of date when anything it was made from changes: splits, details, products or heat. (smoke test)
9. **Gut cap (RD-02-A1, RP-02-A2).** Planned carbs per hour, drink and gels together, never pass the gut figure on any leg, for any product. A drink too strong on its own is diluted with water, with a warning. (`fuel.test.js`)
10. **Fluid cap (RD-02-W1).** Planned fluid never passes the sweat rate. (`fuel.test.js`)
11. **Own products (RD-02-A3).** People can add, edit and remove gels, chews and drinks. The starter products are generic, with no brand names. Carbs per serve is picked from a list of 20 to 50 g in 5 g steps, or typed exactly under Other. A saved figure that is not on the list shows as it is under Other and is never rounded to a step. On the fuel step the athlete picks the carbs, not the product (RD-009, decided 4 Nov 2026): a gel of 20, 25, 30 or 40g, and a drink of 20, 30, 40 or 60g per serve or water only, with the unit in the label. The product's own figure from My products is offered too, so a label that says 22g stays selectable. The pick replaces the product's carbs only. The plan keeps the first gel's and first drink's name and sodium, and the pick is part of the plan input, so a saved plan goes out of date when it changes. A pick never lifts the gut cap. The product a person picks is named in the fuel plan rows and in the race card text, so the choice shows where it is used (the 70 mm print card is unchanged). A line under the choice on the fuel step says which gel and drink the plan uses and their carbs per serve, with the unit joined to the figure (25g a serve, 30g per 500 ml), and leaves out a figure it cannot read. The default products are named plainly (Gel, Sports drink, Chews, 3 pieces) with no "(typical)" in the name, and an older save that still has the tag loads without it. A fuel plan saved before the tag was dropped still holds the tagged names, so the race card, the fuel step rows and the carry list show them plain (`plainName` in `fuel.js`; `fuel.test.js`, smoke test). The fuel card says they are generic starters until the person edits their products. The ranges behind them stay marked unchecked. (smoke test)
12. **Not medical advice (RD-02-A2)** appears beside every plan, on the card and on the print.
13. **Guide embeds (RD-02-A4, F7, RP-07).** A guide page runs one step on its own with no race set up. "Save to a race plan" starts a plan and carries the guide's numbers in. (smoke test)
14. **Printable top-tube card (RD-07-A2).** Print shows only a card about 7 cm wide with pace targets and fuel times. (smoke test)
15. **Phone first.** No sideways scroll at 390 px. Tap targets at least 44 px. Text goes into the page as text, never as HTML. Labels in the fuel plan rows wrap at spaces only and are never broken in the middle of a word, at 390, 360 and 320 px (smoke test). The date and start time fields on the first set-up screen sit side by side inside the page with a gap at 390, 360 and 320 px (smoke test, which runs in Chromium only; the original overflow was seen in iOS Safari and needs a check on a phone).
16. **Splash start (RD-033, replaces the blank card start of 4 Nov 2026; decided 6 Nov 2026).** The first screen, for a race with no name, date, pace or fuel, is a splash: the TRI Momentum logo (reversed: navy on light), the line "Race with a plan.", one sentence on what the app is for, and four numbered steps in order (Choose a race, Choose pacing, Review nutrition, Get your race card for your top tube; the last is shaded 10% darker than the page). Under them: "Start my race card" (the one yellow action), "Just plan my fuel" as a link, and the line "Free. No account. Saved on this phone only. Typical ranges, not medical advice." There is no sign-in: the app has no accounts. Countdown and Debrief are not mentioned. Cancel on step 1 returns to it. The guides sit below as gallery cards: one column on a phone, two across when there is room, each at least 44 px high, each tagged Draft while its ranges are unchecked. It fits 390, 360 and 320 px with every action at least 44 px high. The logo is the owner's own; the design is the D2 Light option, 6 Nov 2026. (smoke test)
17. **Optional race times (RD-019, decided 4 Nov 2026).** Start time and Transition opens are both optional and start blank. A blank time is never read as midnight, and 00:00 is a real time. When both are set, the transition opens before the start. If not, a message shows by the field, the entry is not saved and Next stays put. Each time can be cleared. A time shows on the home screen, the race card and the print card only when it is set, and never as a label with nothing after it. (`time.test.js`; smoke test)
18. **Km beside gel times (RD-025).** Each gel time on the bike and the run shows about how far into the leg it is, marked "~", to the whole km (`kmAt` in `pace.js`, beside the leg distances): gel time ÷ leg time × leg distance, at an even pace. The time comes first and stays the rule. The km shows in the plan rows, the race card (each leg is a card, each gel a row with its time first and its km beside it, the leg's carbs an hour beside its name, as the 4 Nov 2026 timeline blueprint), the print card and the run line in the guide embed, and wherever it shows so does the line "Km are a guide, from an even pace. Go by the clock: never take a gel earlier than its time." (`KM_NOTE` in `pace.js`; the owner has not yet approved the wording). A leg time that is blank, unreadable or zero shows the time alone, never a km or "NaN". A km never passes the leg distance. Gel times, their count, carbs per hour and the caps do not change. (`pace.test.js`, `fuel.test.js`; smoke test)
19. **Step bar (RD-031, decided 5 Nov 2026).** Every step of a race plan (F1 to F6, the fuel step) and the race card carry a floating step bar: Race, Pacing, Fuel and Card, each an icon with its name, at least 44 px, and in view as the page scrolls. The current step is marked, and a done step has a green dot and says ", done" to a screen reader. Any step opens from any other: Pacing opens the targets (F5) once pace is saved, and the set-up start (F2) before that. Once the card has a plan, steps F1 to F4 have "Save and back to race card", which works the pace targets out again. A second save on F5 goes back to the card; only the first goes to F6. Pace targets keep what they were made from, so the card says "Your numbers changed since these targets" when a person changes a number and leaves without saving. A plan saved before this check existed is never called out of date for that alone. The bar replaces the old "1 Pace / 2 Fuel" bar and is not on the home screen, the products screen or the fuel tool used with no race. (smoke test)
20. **Taper length (RD-011, RD-031).** Under the countdown on F1: 10, 14 or 21 days as one tap, with our guide marked (14 for a 70.3, 10 for a Standard), or pick the day the taper starts. A picked day must fall 1 to 42 days before race day; any other day is refused with a message, stays on screen, and is not saved. Choosing our guide saves blank, never a number. The home screen and the race card show when the taper starts, or "In your taper". The print card does not: it is for race day. The guide and limits sit in `TAPER` in `pace.js`, marked UNCHECKED. (`pace.test.js` "taperDays"; smoke test)
21. **Your own T1 and T2 (RD-022, RD-031).** F5 shows T1 and T2 as their own rows, each marked "Our guide" or "Your time", and two fields under the targets. A time is whole minutes, 1 to 30; a blank uses our guide and is never zero; anything else shows a message by the field, the plan uses our guide for it, and Save waits. The finish is still exactly the legs plus the changeovers. Our split guide (`T1`, `T2` in `pace.js`, UNCHECKED) adds up to the old combined range, so a plan with no times of its own is unchanged. The race card shows the changeovers its finish includes. (`pace.test.js` "your own T1 and T2", "a T1 or T2 that is not whole minutes"; smoke test)
22. **Gel timing (RD-031).** The fuel step has a Gel timing card: for the bike and the run, "first gel at" (h:mm) and "then every" (whole minutes, 5 to 120), with our suggested times as the placeholders and written out underneath. Both blank uses our suggested times; one blank asks for both. No gel goes in the last 10 minutes of a leg. Times that would take carbs past the gut figure on a leg are refused with a message by the fields, and that leg keeps the suggested times: the gut cap never moves. "Use the suggested times" clears them. The athlete's times show in the plan rows, the carry list, the race card and the print card, each with its km. They are part of the plan input, so a saved plan goes out of date when they change; a plan saved before they existed does not. The race card's fuel section links to them ("Change gel times"). (`fuel.test.js` "your own gel times"; smoke test)

## Out of scope

Payments and the paywall (D6), email, including "Email as PDF" from the race card, which waits on D4 (5 Nov 2026), the gut-training ladder, Countdown, Debrief, an offline copy (RD-07-S1), analytics (D3), hosting and deployment, the SEO pipeline, and published guide copy. Countdown and Debrief are hidden everywhere (the card, the home plan list and the fuel step bar) until they exist, so nothing promises a tool that is not there (decided 4 Nov 2026).

## Known limits

- Every range in `pace.js` and `fuel.js` is typical and **unchecked** (RP-05-W1, RD-01-W2). Verify each before launch.
- The bike time comes from a simple physics model (CdA 0.29, Crr 0.005). It lands near, not on, the blueprint example: 2:47–2:51 against 2:41–2:48.
- One race at a time. "Start a new race" replaces it and keeps the details.
- The strategy sequences Pacing after Gate 1. This build makes both now, at the owner's request (3 Nov 2026).
