Helping Triathletes shift gear

Strong calves make durable legs. Change shoes gradually.

Straight-leg and bent-knee calf loading protects the Achilles and soleus against running impact. Rotate new footwear and trail surfaces slowly over weeks.

CALF LOADING Straight-leg & bent-knee weighted calf raises

EQUIPMENT SHIFT Mix new shoe drop & trail camber across 3–4 wks
