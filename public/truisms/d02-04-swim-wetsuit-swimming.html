Helping Triathletes shift gear

Fit beats brand. Practise the strip.

A tight suit constricts breathing; a loose suit scoops cold water. Try before you buy, test it in open water, and rehearse peeling it at speed for T1.

01 · FIT BEATS BRAND Check fit swimming, not standing. Snug chest, zero water scoop at neck, complete arm rotation freedom.
SHOULDER MOBILITY
02 · ACCLIMATISATION Swim in cold water before race week. Adjust to neoprene chest compression so panic never takes hold.
REHEARSE FIRST
TRANSITION SPEED · SAVE 30 SECONDS Unzip on the exit run, pull arms clear, and strip to waist while running. Seconds disappear when neoprene sticks at the ankles.
PRACTISE THE STRIP
