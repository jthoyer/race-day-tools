Race-day tools

How it works

Why this app exists

Draft. The ranges the app uses are typical values that have not yet been checked against current sources.

Race day asks a lot of your head. This app takes what you know about yourself and turns it into a plan you can read on the bike.

You get pace targets for each leg, a fuel plan with times, and one race card to print for your top tube.

What it will and won’t do

  • It gives you a range, never one time. Racing is not that exact.
  • It never plans more carbs an hour than the gut figure you enter.
  • It never plans more fluid than your sweat rate.
  • It sends nothing anywhere. No account, no server, no analytics.
  • It is not medical advice.

From a blank card to a printed one

  1. Start. Tap Start my race card. Name your race, pick the distance and the date. A start time is optional.
  2. About you. Enter your weight, your sweat rate (or use a typical one) and the carbs an hour your gut can handle.
  3. Your numbers. Add your swim speed, then power, heart rate or effort for the bike and run. Pick the course and the heat.
  4. Pace. Check your targets for each leg. The finish is a range, and the legs add up to it. Save them.
  5. Fuel. Tap Plan my fuel. Pick the carbs in your gel and drink. Keep the suggested gel times, or set your own.
  6. Print. Open the race card and tap Print top-tube card.

Only need the fuel?

Tap Just plan my fuel on the first screen. You can add a pace later. Or start with a guide:

  • How many gels for a 70.3?
  • How hard should you ride a 70.3 bike?

Where your details live

On this phone, in this browser. Nobody else can see them. If you clear your browser data, they go. Another phone starts blank.
