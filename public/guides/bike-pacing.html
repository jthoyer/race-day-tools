How hard should you ride a 70.3 bike?

Draft guide. The ranges below are typical values that have not yet been checked against current sources. Review before publishing.

Most age-groupers do best at about three-quarters of their FTP. Ride harder and the run pays for it. Try your own numbers below.

Why the run decides it

Your run starts tired. A bike held at the low end of the range often gives you back more time on the run than it costs you on the bike.

What changes the target

Hills: on a hilly course, aim a little lower on average, because the climbs pull your power up. Heat: on a hot day, ease off a little more. An old FTP test: if it is more than six weeks old, retest or aim lower.

Typical ranges, not medical advice.
