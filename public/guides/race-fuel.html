How many gels for a 70.3?

Draft guide. The ranges below are typical values that have not yet been checked against current sources. Review before publishing.

It depends on how long you are out there and how much your gut has learned to handle. The answer is never more than you have practised. Try your own numbers below.

Train your gut first

The plan never asks for more carbs an hour than you enter. If your number is low, build it up on long rides over several weeks, then raise it here.

Don’t drink more than you sweat

Drinking more than you lose can dilute the sodium in your blood, which is dangerous. The plan keeps your fluid under your sweat rate. Test your sweat rate on a one-hour ride to make it fit you.

Typical ranges, not medical advice.
